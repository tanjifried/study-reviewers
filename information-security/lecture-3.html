Statistical randomness testing

NIST SP 800-22 is a suite of 15 statistical tests used to look for detectable patterns in binary sequences. It evaluates whether sequences appear sufficiently random for cryptographic use.

Runs

A run is a consecutive group of identical bits. The runs test examines the number of runs in a sequence.

Hypotheses

The null hypothesis H₀ is that the sequence is random under the test model. The alternative Hₐ is that it is not.

Count the bits

Count ones as N1 and zeros as N2. For 1001101110100011, N1 = 9 and N2 = 7.

Count runs

Split 1001101110100011 into 1 | 00 | 11 | 0 | 111 | 0 | 1 | 000 | 11. There are five runs of ones and four runs of zeros, for nine total.

Compare and interpret

The document gives critical values 4 and 14 for its example and compares 4 < 9 < 14. It therefore fails to reject H₀. The document concludes that the sequence is random.
