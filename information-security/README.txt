INFORMATION ASSURANCE AND SECURITY STUDY PACK

Open index.html in a browser. Keep this entire folder together so the slides, PDFs, and quiz links work. The pack works offline after the files are available locally; the external NIST reference links require internet access.

The pack contains only the two requested source presentations: Authentication and Avalanche Effect Computation. Each lecture contains slide images, plain-language explanations, examples, a cheat sheet, and a link to its own quiz.

The quizzes are multiple choice only (no true/false) and focus on key definitions and important details rather than worked examples. Each lesson quiz has one question per concept (20 and 14); the final has 34 questions covering both lessons. Feedback links to the related lecture section. Press Enter after a correct answer to advance. Completed scores and progress are stored separately from the Software Engineering pack in the current browser when storage is available.

The slide comparison contains an arithmetic error: its two displayed ciphertexts differ by 30 of 128 bits, or 23.4375%, not 31 bits. The underlying encryption parameters are not specified, so the ciphertext cannot be reproduced from the slide alone. The SHA-1 properties slide also has formula typos. Corrections and references appear in the lesson.

The lessons are study aids, not official examination questions. Original PowerPoints are in lessons/ and PDF copies are in pdfs/.
