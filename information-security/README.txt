INFORMATION ASSURANCE AND SECURITY STUDY PACK

Open index.html in a browser. Keep the folder together for offline use. The pack includes Authentication, Avalanche Effect Computation, and Statistical Randomness Testing. The original materials are in lessons/ and extracted text is in source-text/.

Each quiz groups multiple-choice questions followed by typed identification questions, ordered by source presentation or lecture. Identification accepts case and punctuation differences; use the displayed term. The final exam draws from all lessons. Progress is stored in your browser.

The avalanche and SHA-1 lesson corrections are documented in those lessons.
