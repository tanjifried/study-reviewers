SLIDE 3
AVALANCHE EFFECT
The 
avalanche effect
 refers to a phenomenon where a small change in input leads to a significant change in output.
The 
avalanche effect
 is a property of cryptographic algorithms where a small change in input (like flipping a single bit) causes a significant change in output.
