# Software Engineering source wording

The 180 concept rows were checked against their cited slides. Definitions now use slide terminology and short source clauses, with modest grammar changes for standalone answers. False statements change or reverse a fact from the relevant topic; helpful concept labels and existing slide references remain in place. The generated question bank and quiz retain their original order and answer positions.

A few compact labels describe material expressed indirectly in the slides: the W5HH “Who and Where” and “How and How Much” distinctions expand slide 8's broad “resource allocation and execution” wording; the Collaborative Innovation slide 9 identifies the Development Team as self-organizing and cross-functional without defining those terms in detail; and the GitHub slide 10 names the base and compare branches without defining each selection. These explanations use the ordinary meaning of those terms to make the answers useful. The Agile role vocabulary and PEP 8 figures are presented as described in the teaching slides rather than as a claim that they represent every current convention. False statements are deliberately incorrect assessment distractors.
