SOFTWARE ENGINEERING STUDY PACK

Open index.html in a modern browser. Start with a lecture, review its cheat sheet, then follow the link at the end to its quiz. The index also links to the combined final exam.

The lessons/ folder holds the original PowerPoints; pdfs/ holds the converted PDFs; slide-images/ holds the rendered PDF pages. Keep these folders beside the HTML files for all links and images to work.

Each lesson keeps a multiple-choice question for every concept and includes fewer true/false questions. Existing attempts retain their original question count until restarted. The final exam has 180 questions, one per concept, across all lessons.

Each answer immediately shows the correct choice, an explanation, and a link to the matching section of the HTML lesson. First answers are scored and locked. Press Enter after a correct answer to move to the next question. Results let you start a fresh attempt, while completed scores remain in progress history. Progress saves in your browser when local storage is available. Different browsers or moving the folder may have separate saved progress.

The quiz and plain-language explanations are study aids created from the presentations, not official exam questions. The two differently numbered teamwork presentations have separate lectures and quizzes.

source-text/ and build/ contain the extracted slide text, question bank, lesson explanations, and build scripts.
